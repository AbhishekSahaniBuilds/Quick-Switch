# Quick Switch

### Fast Response, Less Waiting!

A fast, lightweight, browser-based AI chatbot designed to make interacting with AI simple and accessible.

No mandatory login, no signup, and no unnecessary complexity — just open the website, select an AI model, and start chatting.

Powered by **Groq**, **React**, and **FastAPI**, Quick Switch focuses on delivering a responsive AI experience through a simple interface.

> **Our goal:** Make AI assistance faster, lighter, and easier to access.

## Overview

- AI conversations using Groq models.
- Dynamic AI model selection.
- Locally stored conversation history.
- Responsive and lightweight interface.
- Markdown-rendered AI responses.
- Adjustable model temperature.
- No mandatory account or login.
